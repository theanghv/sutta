## Practices

### Establish mindfulness

- **[[Practices/Mindfulness of breathing|Mindfulness of breathing]]** — Train through body, feelings, mind, and letting go with the breath.  
  **Start:** [[Sutta texts/MN 118#^breathing-training|MN 118 — Mindfulness of Breathing]]

- **[[Practices/Mindfulness of the body|Mindfulness of the body]]** — Develop mindfulness through breathing, posture, daily activities, and bodily contemplations.  
  **Start:** [[Sutta texts/MN 119#^body-practice|MN 119 — Mindfulness of the Body]]

- **[[Practices/Satipaṭṭhāna|Satipaṭṭhāna]]** — Explore the four domains of mindfulness and their exercises.  
  **Start:** [[Sutta texts/MN 10#Text|MN 10 — Mindfulness Meditation]]

### Cultivate the heart and mind

- **[[Practices/Mettā and the brahmavihāras|Mettā and the brahmavihāras]]** — Cultivate goodwill, compassion, rejoicing, and equanimity.  
  **Start:** [[Sutta texts/Snp 1.8#Text|Snp 1.8 — The Discourse on Love]]

- **[[Practices/Joy and settling the mind|Joy and settling the mind]]** — Understand how joy, rapture, tranquility, and bliss support meditation.  
  **Start:** [[Sutta texts/AN 11.2#^joy-and-settling|AN 11.2 — Making a Wish]]

### Work with difficulties

- **[[Practices/Energy (Viriya)|Energy — Viriya]]** — Work with dullness and drowsiness, and cultivate balanced energy.  
  **Start:** [[Sutta texts/SN 46.51#^nourishing-energy|SN 46.51 — Nourishing]]

- **[[Practices/Abandoning unskillful thoughts|Abandoning unskillful thoughts]]** — Recognize harmful thinking, change its direction, and settle the mind.  
  **Start:** [[Sutta texts/MN 19#^two-kinds-of-thought|MN 19 — Two Kinds of Thought]]

- **[[Practices/Asubha|Asubha]]** — Contemplate unattractiveness as a way of addressing lust.  
  **Start:** [[Sutta texts/MN 62#^asubha|MN 62 — The Longer Advice to Rāhula]]

### Investigate experience and let go

- **[[Practices/Dependent origination|Dependent origination]]** — Understand the conditions for suffering, their cessation, and the conditions supporting freedom.  
  **Start:** [[Sutta texts/SN 12.2#^twelve-links|SN 12.2 — Analysis]]

- **[[Practices/Element contemplation|Element contemplation]]** — Examine internal and external elements and relinquish ownership.  
  **Start:** [[Sutta texts/MN 62#^elements|MN 62 — The Longer Advice to Rāhula]]

- **[[Practices/Feelings and impermanence|Feelings and impermanence]]** — Recognize pleasant, painful, and neutral feeling and examine change.  
  **Start:** [[Sutta texts/MN 10#2. Observing the Feelings|MN 10 — Mindfulness Meditation]]

- **[[Practices/Contemplation of the aggregates|Contemplation of the aggregates]]** — Examine the five aggregates, grasping, and identification as self.  
  **Start:** [[Sutta texts/SN 22.1#^training|SN 22.1 — Nakula’s Father]]

- **[[Practices/Consciousness and the six senses|Consciousness and the six senses]]** — Examine knowing itself, its conditions, and its changing nature.  
  **Start:** [[Sutta texts/MN 148#^six-kinds-of-consciousness|MN 148 — Six By Six]]

## Reading and notes

Each source note preserves Sujato’s published translation, including its abbreviated repetitions, and links back to SuttaCentral. Practice summaries, catalogue reminders, and reading routes are added notes. Sujato’s commentary is explicitly attributed where included. The personal reflection sections are available for your own observations.
