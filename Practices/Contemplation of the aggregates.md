Examining form, feeling, perception, choices or volitional formations, and consciousness: how they arise and cease, how grasping relates to them, and why they are to be seen as not-self.

## What each discourse contributes

- [[Sutta texts/SN 22.1#^training|SN 22.1 — Nakula’s Father]] — The practical application of not-self: an ailing body with a healthy mind. Explains how identification with each aggregate leads to distress when it changes. Includes a [[Sutta texts/SN 22.1#Four ways of relating self to form|guide to the four self-views]].

- [[Sutta texts/SN 22.59#Text|SN 22.59 — The Characteristic of Not-Self]] — The central contemplation of each aggregate as impermanent, liable to affliction, and not fit to be regarded as mine, me, or my self.

- [[Sutta texts/SN 22.56#Text|SN 22.56 — Rounds of the Grasping Aggregates]] — Defines each aggregate and examines its origin, cessation, and the path leading to cessation.

- [[Sutta texts/SN 22.95#Text|SN 22.95 — A Lump of Foam]] — Provides the similes of foam, a bubble, a mirage, a banana trunk, and a magic trick for closely examining the aggregates and their lack of a substantial core.

- [[Sutta texts/MN 109#^grasping|MN 109 — The Longer Discourse on the Full-Moon Night]] — Clarifies the relation between the aggregates and grasping: desire and greed for the aggregates is the grasping there. It also examines their conditions and the ways self-identification arises.

- [[Sutta texts/MN 28#^form-and-elements|MN 28 — The Longer Simile of the Elephant’s Footprint]] — Connects element contemplation with the form aggregate and later explains how the aggregates gather in conditioned sense experience.

- [[Sutta texts/MN 10#4.2. The Aggregates|MN 10 — Mindfulness Meditation]] — Gives the concise satipaṭṭhāna instruction to know each aggregate and its arising and passing away.


- [[Sutta texts/MN 35#^self-and-mastery|MN 35 — The Shorter Discourse With Saccaka]] — Makes the relation between a self claim and mastery explicit through the analogy of a ruler’s authority in their realm, then tests the claim against all five aggregates.

- [[Sutta texts/MN 44#^grasping|MN 44 — The Shorter Elaboration]] — Clarifies that grasping is the desire and greed for the aggregates, and examines ways self-identification occurs.

- [[Sutta texts/SN 18.10#Text|SN 18.10 — The Aggregates]] — The short aggregate examination in the SN 18 insight framework.

- [[Sutta texts/SN 18.21#Text|SN 18.21 — Tendency]] — Examines the aggregates in relation to I-making, mine-making, and the underlying tendency to conceit.

- [[Sutta texts/SN 18.22#Text|SN 18.22 — Rid of Conceit]] — Connects seeing the aggregates as not-self with the heart’s freedom through not grasping.
