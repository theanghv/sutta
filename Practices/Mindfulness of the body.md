Establishing mindfulness with the body through breathing, posture, everyday activities, bodily parts, elements, and contemplation of the body’s changing condition.

## What each discourse contributes

- [[Sutta texts/MN 119#^body-practice|MN 119 — Mindfulness of the Body]] — The main reference for this practice. Brings together the body exercises, describes the settling of the mind, and develops the account through the absorptions and the benefits of sustained practice.

- [[Sutta texts/MN 10#1. Observing the Body|MN 10 — Mindfulness Meditation]] — Sets out the body exercises within satipaṭṭhāna, with recurring attention to arising and passing away and to dwelling without grasping.
