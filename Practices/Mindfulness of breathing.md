Training with the in-breath and out-breath: knowing breathing, experiencing and calming the body, and developing the training through feelings, mind, and letting go.

## What each discourse contributes

- [[Sutta texts/MN 118#^breathing-training|MN 118 — Mindfulness of Breathing]] — The main reference for the sixteen breathing steps. Later in the discourse, each group of four is connected with a domain of mindfulness, followed by the awakening factors and freedom.

- [[Sutta texts/MN 62#^breathing-training|MN 62 — The Longer Advice to Rāhula]] — Gives the sixteen breathing steps within an instruction that also includes element contemplation, the brahmavihāras, and other meditations.

- [[Sutta texts/MN 10#1.1. Mindfulness of Breathing|MN 10 — Mindfulness Meditation]] — Places the first four breathing steps within contemplation of the body and the wider practice of satipaṭṭhāna.

- [[Sutta texts/MN 119#^body-practice|MN 119 — Mindfulness of the Body]] — Begins the body practice with the first four breathing steps, then develops other ways of attending to the body and describes collectedness and absorption.


- [[Sutta texts/MN 44#^three-processes|MN 44 — The Shorter Elaboration]] — Defines breathing as a physical process and perception and feeling as a mental process, supplying useful context for those terms in the breathing instructions.
