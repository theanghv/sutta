The four domains of mindfulness: body, feelings, mind, and principles. These discourses provide the wider context in which several practices in this collection sit.

## What each discourse contributes

- [[Sutta texts/MN 10#Text|MN 10 — Mindfulness Meditation]] — The main reference for the four domains and their exercises, including breathing, postures, elements, feelings, mind states, hindrances, and aggregates.

- [[Sutta texts/MN 118#^breathing-and-mindfulness|MN 118 — Mindfulness of Breathing]] — Explains how the four groups of breathing instructions fulfill the four domains of mindfulness, and how mindfulness develops the awakening factors.


- [[Sutta texts/MN 44#^immersion|MN 44 — The Shorter Elaboration]] — Defines immersion as unification of mind, names the four kinds of mindfulness meditation as its bases, and the four right efforts as its prerequisites.
