Contemplation of the body’s unattractive aspects as a way of addressing lust and attachment to bodily appearance.

## What each discourse contributes

- [[Sutta texts/MN 62#^asubha|MN 62 — The Longer Advice to Rāhula]] — States the practical purpose directly: meditation on unattractiveness is given as a way of abandoning lust.

- [[Sutta texts/MN 10#1.4. Focusing on the Repulsive|MN 10 — Mindfulness Meditation]] — Provides contemplation of bodily parts; the later charnel-ground section adds contemplation of the body’s decay.

- [[Sutta texts/MN 119#^body-parts|MN 119 — Mindfulness of the Body]] — Includes bodily-parts and charnel-ground contemplations within the cultivation of mindfulness of the body.

- [[Sutta texts/AN 4.163#Text|AN 4.163 — Ugly]] — Places this contemplation among several practices and distinguishes painful and pleasant practice with slow or swift insight. It gives context for its role among different ways of practice.
