A framework for the insight side of this collection: apply the same examination of impermanence, suffering, and not-self across different aspects of experience. The practice notes supply fuller instructions and explanations for the areas this series names briefly.

[[Sutta texts/SN 18.1#Text|SN 18.1 — The Eye, Etc.]] begins with Rāhula asking for a short teaching so he can live alone, withdrawn, diligent, keen, and resolute. That makes it a useful starting point for retreat preparation and a compact reference to return to during practice.

## First cycle: SN 18.1–10

The recurring examination asks whether each aspect is permanent, whether what is impermanent is suffering or happiness, and whether what is impermanent, suffering, and perishable is fit to regard as mine, me, or my self. The movement is toward disillusionment, fading desire, and freedom.
